# Visual style guide (v2)

**Mood:** rainy noir city, warm interiors against cool streets. Readable first, moody second: gameplay objects are never lost in shadow.

**Scale:** logical canvas 640×360, integer scaling with `image-rendering: pixelated`. Tile unit 16px. Characters 24×32 (≈2 tiles tall). Props are drawn at final scale, never upscaled.

**Pixel rules:** no anti-aliasing, no blur, no sub-pixel placement (camera and sprites snap to integers). Every sprite has a 1px dark selective outline tinted toward the local colour (never pure black). Light comes from the **upper left**: highlight top-left, shadow bottom-right, rim light on wet surfaces.

**Characters:** each recurring character has a unique **silhouette**, not just a colour: Ward — long trench coat and hair bun; Brooks — police cap, broad shoulders, duty belt, badge; Player — fedora and leather jacket, notebook; Vance — hooded sweatshirt with beanie; Marla — long hair and cardigan; Hale — suit, glasses, briefcase. Sprite spec: 24×32 frame, feet anchored bottom-centre at (12,31), left-facing frames mirrored for right. Poses: idle(2) walk(4) talk(2) react(2) inspect(3) read(2) search(4) handcuff(3) present(2) cuffed(2) type(2).

**Animation timing:** inspect 0.4–0.8 s · evidence pickup 0.5–1.0 s · door 0.4–0.9 s · search 1.5–3 s · arrest 2–4 s · discovery 0.6–1.2 s. Everything skippable; previously seen sequences run at 2× automatically.

**Lighting:** ambient overlay ≤ 0.4 outdoors, ≤ 0.2 indoors. Light sources: street lamps, lit windows, headlights, patrol flasher (red/blue alternating, additive). Rain, splashes and puddle ripples are particles, capped (≤140 drops) and disabled by reduced-motion.

**Honest status of the art:** generated by code into PNG atlases (original, no third-party assets). It is *benchmark quality*, not final. `docs/ASSET_WISHLIST.md` lists what a human artist or image-generation workflow should replace; the loader consumes any PNG atlas with the same frame names, so replacement needs no engine changes.
